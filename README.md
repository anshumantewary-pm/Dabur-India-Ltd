Financial Analysis of Dabur India Ltd.
Project Overview

This repository contains a comprehensive financial analysis of Dabur India Ltd., one of India’s leading FMCG companies.
The project evaluates Dabur’s financial position, operating performance, financial health, and cost of capital using standard corporate finance frameworks.

The analysis is structured to be academic, MBA-ready, and industry-aligned, making it suitable for finance coursework, valuation studies, and analytical demonstrations.

Company Background
Dabur India Ltd. is a multinational consumer goods company founded in 1884, with a strong presence in:
Healthcare & Ayurveda-based products
Personal care
Food & beverages
International markets across Asia, Africa, and the Middle East

The company follows an asset-light FMCG business model, supported by strong brand equity and stable cash flows.
